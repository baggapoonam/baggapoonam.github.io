## Every Experience Became a Building Block

My career began in business operations, where I developed skills in problem-solving, reporting, stakeholder collaboration, and process improvement. Over time, I became increasingly interested in how information could support better decision-making. Looking back, these experiences laid the foundation for analytical thinking long before I formally studied data analytics.

Curiosity led me to explore data analytics, furthermore, learn visualization, and explore Artificial Intelligence. Today, I combine practical business experience with analytical tools to uncover insights, solve problems, and support informed decision-making.
